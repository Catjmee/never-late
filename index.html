Neverlate

One step at a time. Neverlate keeps track of the rest.

A calm personal assistant for getting out of the door. It plans backwards from when you need to be seated, then walks you through each step as the clock reaches it.

Try Neverlate Read the product brief

The problem

Time doesn’t vanish in the big moments. It slips away in the gaps between them.

Alarms wake you up and sat nav tells you how long the drive is. Each tool helps with one moment, but none of them talk to each other.

The minutes that go missing sit between the major stages: finishing getting ready, finding your keys, walking to the platform, signing in at reception, finding the right room. Nobody plans for those, so they quietly add up to being late.

For anyone with time blindness, keeping all of that in your head is exhausting. It turns every trip into a source of stress before you’ve even left the house.

Key features

It takes the thinking out of planning and timekeeping

  • Plans backwards from your seat

    Tell it when something starts. It works out when to start getting ready, when to leave and when to be seated, 5 minutes early.

  • One step on screen at a time

    A live countdown for the step you’re on, with a short checklist like shower, hair and make-up. Nothing else to hold in your head.

  • Moves on when you do

    Leave the house and it starts your travel step for you. Get near the venue and it switches to the walk in.

  • Plan the week from your calendar

    Events come in from Outlook and Teams. Add a plan to each one from a preset, so a busy week is sorted in one sitting.

  • Calm when things slip

    Running behind? It trims spare time first, tells you your new arrival time and drafts a message. No alarms that flash and no blame.

  • Learns where your time goes

    After each trip it asks how it went and adjusts your plan, so the timings fit your real life a little better every time.

  1. Plan

    Pick your usual steps once. Rough guesses are fine.

  2. Go

    Follow one step at a time, with prompts at the level you choose.

  3. Check in

    Early, on time or late? One tap, plus what slowed you down.

  4. Fine-tune

    Your plan adjusts, so you can see where time is lost and win it back.

Why it exists

Neverlate began with its creator’s own time blindness, and the stress of always running to catch up.

The idea is simple. You shouldn’t have to be the one tracking the clock, juggling apps and doing mental maths while trying to get out of the door.

Neverlate is a personal assistant for that part of your day. It keeps track of everything else, so you can focus on the one step in front of you.
