# Neverlate

An iPhone app that plans every step between now and an event's start time, so you arrive at your seat on time and settled.

- Product requirements: [docs/PRD.md](docs/PRD.md)
- Clickable prototype: [prototype/index.html](prototype/index.html). Open it in a browser at phone width. It covers onboarding, Today, calendar, plan builder, alarm, getting ready, leaving checklist, at your seat, arrival check-in and evening-before prep, in the dark Lagoon teal palette.
- Colour options compared: [design/colour-options.html](design/colour-options.html)
- Figma imports: [design/figma/](design/figma/), SVG exports of earlier screens in the older coral palette

## Accessibility

Built to WCAG 2.2 AA. Text contrast is 5.8:1 or higher and control outlines are 3.7:1 or higher. Every status pairs an icon with words, tap targets are at least 44px, and nothing flashes.
