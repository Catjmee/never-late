Pick a colour for Never Late

Each option shows the new alarm as one large, steady block of colour, with no flashing. Next to it is the Today screen in the same palette. Text is a soft off-white rather than pure white, and the accent appears in one main place per screen. Every pairing passes WCAG AA contrast, with the lowest at 7.7:1.

How the alarm behaves in every option

The colour stays steady and never flashes or pulses. Sound and vibration follow your prompt level, and VoiceOver reads the step aloud. The main button is the largest thing on the screen, and "I need more time" is always one tap away.
